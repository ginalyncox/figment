# Creator License Agreement

**DRAFT — not yet reviewed by a lawyer. Version: draft-2026-11-14.**

This agreement is between you (the "Creator") and Gina Makes LLC, an Iowa limited liability company in Altoona, Iowa ("Gina Makes", "we", "us"). It covers the videos you upload to our video service (the "Service"). By ticking the box and uploading, you agree to it.

## 1. The short version

- You keep ownership of your videos.
- You give us permission (a license) to show them to viewers and charge a subscription for the Service.
- Each month, subscription money is split. We keep a service fee. The rest is shared among creators by how much paying subscribers watched their videos.
- You promise you have the rights to what you upload, and that it follows our content rules.

The short version is a summary only. The sections below are the agreement.

## 2. Who can be a creator

You must be at least 18 years old and able to enter a contract. You must complete payout setup with our payment provider, Stripe, including identity and tax details. [ASK LAWYER: creators outside the United States — allow or not at launch?]

## 3. You keep ownership

You keep all ownership rights you have in your videos. This agreement does not transfer ownership to us.

## 4. The license you give us

You give Gina Makes a **non-exclusive, worldwide license** to do these things with each video you upload, for as long as the video is on the Service:

1. Host, store, copy, and convert the video into the formats needed for streaming.
2. Stream and display the video to viewers of the Service, including a limited number of free views offered to people who have not subscribed.
3. Use the video's title, description, thumbnail, your creator display name, and short clips of up to [30] seconds to promote the Service.

"Non-exclusive" means you may post and sell the same video anywhere else.

We may let our service providers (for example video hosting and payment companies) do these things for us. We may not sell or sublicense your video to anyone else.

The license for a video ends when the video is removed from the Service, except that (a) we have up to [30] days to finish removing it from our systems and backups, and (b) we may keep a private copy and records where the law requires it or to handle a dispute.

## 5. How you get paid

**5.1 Definitions.**

- "Net Subscription Revenue" for a calendar month means the subscription payments we actually receive from viewers that month, minus refunds, chargebacks, sales or similar taxes, and payment processing fees.
- "Service Fee" means [20]% of Net Subscription Revenue.
- "Creator Pool" means Net Subscription Revenue minus the Service Fee.
- "Qualified Watch Time" means time that paying subscribers spend actually playing a published video, as measured by our systems. It does not include free views by non-subscribers, your own views of your own videos, or views we reasonably believe are automated, paid-for, or otherwise fake.

**5.2 Your share.** For each calendar month:

> Your payment = Creator Pool × (Qualified Watch Time on your videos ÷ Qualified Watch Time on all videos)

Calendar months and all times are measured in UTC.

**5.3 When.** We calculate payments after each month ends and send them through Stripe within [30] days after the end of that month. Stripe then pays out to your bank on its own schedule.

**5.4 Payout setup.** We can only pay you once your Stripe payout account is active. If it is not, we hold your earnings until it is. [ASK LAWYER: how long may we hold unclaimed earnings, and what happens to them? State unclaimed-property rules may apply.]

**5.5 Adjustments.** If a payment you were paid a share of is later refunded or charged back, or we find that watch time was fake, we may deduct the related amount from your future payments.

**5.6 No guarantee.** We do not promise any minimum number of views or amount of earnings. Our watch-time measurements are final unless there is a clear error. Tell us about a suspected error within [60] days of the payment.

**5.7 Taxes.** You are an independent creator, not our employee, partner, or agent. You are responsible for your own taxes. We or Stripe may collect tax information from you and send tax forms (such as a Form 1099) to you and to tax authorities where the law requires.

## 6. Your promises about each video

Each time you upload, you promise that:

1. **You made it and have the rights.** You created the video, or you have all rights and permissions needed to grant the license in Section 4.
2. **Your AI tools allow it.** The terms of every AI tool you used allow you to use the output commercially in this way.
3. **It is honestly labeled.** The video was made with AI tools, and what you tell us about the tools used is true.
4. **No real people without consent.** The video does not show, imitate, or use the face, voice, name, or likeness of any real person, living or dead, unless you have that person's written consent (or their estate's) and can show it to us on request.
5. **No sexual content.** The video contains no nudity and no sexual or intimate content of any kind. [DECISION FOR GINA: this draft bans all such content at launch, which keeps the Service out of a much riskier legal category.]
6. **Nothing involving minors in a sexual or exploitative way.** This is absolute. We report apparent child sexual abuse material to the authorities as the law requires.
7. **It does not copy others.** The video does not infringe anyone's copyright, trademark, or other rights. This includes recognizable characters, logos, music, and footage owned by others.
8. **It is not harmful or illegal.** The video is not unlawful, defamatory, harassing, or hateful. It does not falsely show a real event or a real person saying or doing something in a way meant to deceive. It does not encourage violence or dangerous acts.
9. **No cheating.** You will not use bots, paid views, or any other trick to inflate watch time.

## 7. AI and copyright

You understand that under current United States law, material generated entirely by AI, without enough human creative input, may not be protected by copyright. The license in Section 4 covers whatever rights you have in the video. Your promises in Section 6 apply whether or not the video is protected by copyright.

## 8. Review and removal

We may review videos before they are published. We may refuse, remove, or restrict any video at any time if we reasonably believe it breaks this agreement, the law, or our policies, or if we receive a removal request about it. We follow our Copyright Policy and our Image Removal Policy. We may end the accounts of creators who repeatedly infringe.

## 9. Ending this agreement

- **By you.** You may remove any video, or close your creator account, at any time.
- **By us.** We may suspend or end your creator account if you break this agreement, or with [30] days' notice for any reason.
- **Earnings.** We will still pay what you earned for Qualified Watch Time before removal or closure, on the normal schedule. We may withhold earnings connected to fake watch time or to videos that broke Section 6.
