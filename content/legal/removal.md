# Image Removal Policy

**DRAFT — not yet reviewed by a lawyer. Version: draft-2026-11-14.**

This policy explains how to ask Gina Makes LLC to remove a video that shows you in an intimate way without your consent, or that uses your face or voice without your consent. It applies to real footage and to AI-made ("deepfake") content.

## 1. Our rule

Nudity and sexual or intimate content are not allowed on the Service at all. Videos may not use a real person's face, voice, or likeness without that person's written consent.

## 2. Intimate images shared without consent

If a video on the Service shows you (or appears to show you) nude or in a sexual or intimate way, and you did not consent to it being published, you can ask us to remove it. A person authorized to act for you can also ask.

**How to ask.** Use the "Report a video" form and choose "Intimate image of me, shared without my consent". Or email [REMOVAL EMAIL]. Your request must include:

1. Your physical or electronic signature (typing your full name counts), or the signature of the person authorized to act for you.
2. The link to the video, or enough detail for us to find it.
3. A short statement that you have a good-faith belief the content was published without your consent.
4. A way to contact you.

**What we do.** We remove the video as soon as possible, and **no later than 48 hours** after we receive a valid request. We also make reasonable efforts to find and remove identical copies. We do this before, not after, any discussion with the creator.

You do not need an account, and there is no charge.

## 3. Your face or voice (not intimate)

If a video uses your face, voice, name, or likeness without your consent, choose "It uses my face or voice without my consent" on the same form. We review these requests within [5] business days and remove the video if the creator cannot show your written consent.

## 4. Minors

If a video shows a person under 18 in a sexual or exploitative way, report it immediately using the form. We remove it, preserve the evidence as the law requires, and report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children. [ASK LAWYER: confirm reporting and preservation duties.]

## 5. Good faith

We may remove content based on a request that appears valid, even if it later turns out the request was mistaken. Do not misuse this process. Knowingly false requests may have legal consequences.
