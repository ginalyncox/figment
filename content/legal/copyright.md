# Copyright Policy

**DRAFT — not yet reviewed by a lawyer. Version: draft-2026-11-14.**

Gina Makes LLC respects the rights of creators and copyright owners. This policy explains how to ask us to remove a video that copies your work, and how a creator can respond. It follows the United States Digital Millennium Copyright Act (the "DMCA", 17 U.S.C. § 512).

## 1. Our copyright agent

Send copyright notices to our designated agent:

- Name: [AGENT NAME OR TITLE]
- Address: [MAILING ADDRESS — this is published by the U.S. Copyright Office, so a business or P.O. box address is common]
- Email: [COPYRIGHT EMAIL]

You can also use the "Report a video" form and choose "It copies my copyrighted work".

## 2. How to send a takedown notice

Your notice must include all of the following:

1. Your physical or electronic signature.
2. A description of the copyrighted work you say was copied.
3. The link to the video on our Service that you want removed.
4. Your name, mailing address, phone number, and email address.
5. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
6. A statement that the information in the notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the copyright owner or are authorized to act for the owner.

## 3. What we do

When we receive a complete notice, we remove or disable the video promptly, tell the creator, and give the creator a copy of the notice.

## 4. Counter-notice (for creators)

If your video was removed and you believe that was a mistake or a misidentification, you may send our agent a counter-notice. It must include:

1. Your physical or electronic signature.
2. The link where the video appeared before it was removed.
3. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that you have a good-faith belief the video was removed because of a mistake or misidentification.
4. Your name, mailing address, and phone number.
5. A statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the federal district court for your address (or, if you are outside the United States, for any district where Gina Makes LLC may be found), and that you will accept service of process from the person who sent the original notice.

We send the counter-notice to the person who complained. Unless they tell us they have filed a court action, we may restore the video 10 to 14 business days after we receive the counter-notice.

## 5. Repeat infringers

We end the accounts of creators who repeatedly infringe. [ASK LAWYER: confirm the standard. This draft uses three valid, uncontested notices.] A creator who receives three valid takedown notices that are not successfully countered will lose their creator account.

## 6. False claims

Under the DMCA, anyone who knowingly makes a false claim in a takedown notice or a counter-notice may be liable for damages. If you are not sure whether your rights are being infringed, consider talking to a lawyer first.
